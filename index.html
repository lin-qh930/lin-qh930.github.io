Qiheng Lin 林琦桁

Ph.D. student in Journalism & Communication

I am a Ph.D. student at Renmin University of China, where my doctoral research focuses on human–AI relations. I work primarily with ethnography and in-depth interviews.

My research explores AI companionship, relationship breakdown, and “AI resurrection.” Across related projects on intimacy and work, I have interviewed more than 100 participants. I am also developing an ethnographic project on how commercial organizations shape human–AI relationships.

Alongside this work, I study gendered labor in influencer industries. In The managed sisterhood (New Media & Society), I examine how sisterly ties organize and govern labor in a Chinese fashion influencer team.
